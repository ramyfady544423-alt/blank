4. Understand the bonding result

Covalent

Atoms share electron pairs. Many main-group atoms tend toward a duet (H) or octet, but there are exceptions.

Ionic

Electrons transfer and oppositely charged ions attract, often forming a crystal lattice rather than a single molecule.

Metallic

Metal atoms form a lattice with delocalized electrons. Metallic bonding is not judged by dividing valence electrons by 8.

Important: “total valence electrons divisible by 8” is not a universal bonding rule. Octet counting is a useful pattern for many simple covalent molecules, but it cannot prove a substance exists or is impossible. Transition metals, odd-electron molecules (NO), expanded-octet species (SF₆), ions, and solids need more detailed chemistry.
