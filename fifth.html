What is viscosity?

Viscosity is a liquid's resistance to flowing. Water has low viscosity; honey, many shampoos and thick sauces have higher viscosity.

Temperature matters
Heating usually makes many liquids thinner, but the amount depends on the substance. This model shows a simplified trend.

Important notes

  • Soap, shampoo, ketchup and sauces can behave non-Newtonian: their apparent viscosity may change when stirred or squeezed.
  • Juices vary depending on pulp, sugar and concentration.
  • Motor oils vary by grade and temperature.
  • Flow speed here is illustrative; it is not a precise engineering calculation.
